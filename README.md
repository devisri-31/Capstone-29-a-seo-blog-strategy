# Capstone 29: AI SEO Blog Strategy for a Niche Website

**Course:** AI Skills Course, Topics 1-10
**Niche:** Free AI tools for college students

## Project question
Can AI help plan keyword-driven blog content without producing low-quality or copied text?

## What this project contains
| # | Item | Summary |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Keyword plan | 9 keywords from Google autocomplete, each with intent and priority |
| 2 | 10 blog topics | Titles generated with ChatGPT, one target keyword each |
| 3 | Article 1 | "10 Free AI Tools I'd Recommend to Any College Student" |
| 4 | Article 2 | "How to Use ChatGPT for College Assignments Without Cheating" |
| 5 | Featured images | Two images designed in Canva |
| 6 | Plagiarism and readability report | Duplichecker, Grammarly and Flesch scores |
| 7 | Outline generator template | Google Sheet that builds an outline from a blog number |
| 8 | Quality comparison | AI-assisted vs unassisted 150-word introduction |
| 9 | Prompt library | 10 reusable prompts |
| 10 | Responsible AI note | How AI was used and checked |

## Tools used
ChatGPT (free), Gemini (free), Grammarly (free), Canva (free), Duplichecker (free), Google Sheets, Google autocomplete.

## AI skills used
Prompt engineering, content writing, research and verification, image generation, automation (Google Sheets outline generator) and responsible AI.

## Key results
| Measure | AI-assisted | Unassisted |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Time taken | about 5 min | 30 min |
| Flesch reading ease | 49.8 | 77.5 |
| Plagiarism | 9% | 8% |
| Overall rating (out of 10) | 6 | 7.5 |

Article plagiarism: Article 1 scored 0% and Article 2 scored 3% in Duplichecker.

## Outline generator template
Google Sheet (view access): https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ri7Rj7DD3zt-8PFvLiqtefoa8OtvuQwSv6f7SsxCJuo/edit?usp=sharing

Type a blog number (1-10) in cell B1 and the title, target keyword and outline update automatically.
